How it works
  • What this is: a one-file phone dashboard for the U.S. (Artemis / commercial HLS) vs China (crewed lunar) race. Fork the scaffold, then replace Unknowns with sourced facts.
  • Home: mission control, both ways to the Moon, the race dial, then the south-pole map. The dial is not the whole page.
  • Tabs: Home · Architecture · Timeline · Path · Media · Claims · Glossary · Sources.
  • Map: filled dots are next-target examples near the pole. Hollow dots on the outer ring are schematic past-landing labels, not polar geography. Tap a row for the site card. Dots are glued to the moon layer.
  • Status symbols: ✓ done · ● on track / in progress · ◐ at risk · ✕ slipped · ? unknown · ○ not started.
  • Dates: hollow ○ = original plan; solid ● = current expectation; the arrow is drift. A dashed slip pill is chrome, not a sourced fact, unless a tag says Official.
  • Pictures: tap a thumbnail to open the official page. 🎨 RENDERING means an artist concept, not a photo.
  • Open the file: download the HTML from chat and use Open with Chrome. If a copy is later stored in Drive, that preview breaks the map and the tabs — download and open in Chrome anyway.
Full details · how to read this seed
  • Architecture panels (Steps, What’s next, Pieces to finish, Compare) are shells. Leave them Unknown until a source says otherwise.
  • Demo CONOPS and crew CONOPS stay in separate bands. Do not copy a tanker count into this file.
  • The Claims tab ships empty on purpose. Fill it after import. Do not paste private notes.

Glossary

Artemis
NASA’s crewed lunar campaign. On Home it is the U.S. path next to commercial HLS.
HLS
Human Landing System. The vehicle that takes crew from lunar orbit to the surface and back.
CLPS
Commercial Lunar Payload Services. NASA program for commercial cargo deliveries to the Moon. Not a crewed landing path in this seed.
CONOPS
Concept of operations. Which flights and which architecture a given mission class uses. Demo and crew stay separate.
Starship HLS
SpaceX lunar lander variant for the Artemis crew-landing path.
Blue Moon
Blue Origin lunar lander. Crewed variant is a separate path from Starship HLS.
New Glenn
Blue Origin heavy launcher. Its own panel in this seed, not a synonym for SLS.
SLS
Space Launch System. NASA heavy-lift rocket that launches Orion.
Orion
NASA crew capsule for Artemis, Earth to lunar orbit.
CMSA
China Manned Space Agency. Crewed spaceflight program (cmse.gov.cn). Lanyue and Mengzhou are credited here.
CNSA
China National Space Administration. National space agency (cnsa.gov.cn).
LM-10
Long March-10. China’s heavy-lift rocket for the crewed lunar path.
Mengzhou
China’s next crewed spacecraft for lunar missions.
Lanyue
China’s crewed lunar lander.
ILRS
International Lunar Research Station. China/Russia-led lunar base concept.
NET
No earlier than. A date floor, not a promise to fly that month.
Depot / tanker
Orbital propellant storage, and the flights that fill it before a lander departs. Counts stay Unknown in this seed.
RENDERING
An artist’s concept, not a photograph of flown hardware.
EXAMPLE
A placeholder row, marker, or counter. Not a sourced fact.
Unknown
Not sourced yet. An Unknown chip is not a fact and not a stand-in for a guess.
Claims log
The tab where sourced statements go after import. Empty in this file.
