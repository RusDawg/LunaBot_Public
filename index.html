Glossary

HLS
Human Landing System — vehicle that takes crew from lunar orbit to the surface and back.
CONOPS
Concept of operations — how many flights / which architecture for a given mission class.
Starship HLS
SpaceX lunar lander variant of Starship for Artemis III / IV.
Blue Moon
Blue Origin lunar lander family; crewed variant for Artemis V path.
SLS
Space Launch System — NASA heavy-lift rocket that launches Orion.
Orion
NASA crew capsule for Artemis Earth ↔ lunar orbit.
LM-10
Long March-10 — China’s heavy-lift rocket for the crewed lunar path.
Mengzhou
China’s next-generation crewed spacecraft for lunar (and LEO) missions.
Lanyue
China’s crewed lunar lander (“embracing the moon”).
ILRS
International Lunar Research Station — China/Russia-led lunar base concept.
NET
No earlier than — a date floor, not a commitment to fly that month.
Depot / tanker
Orbital propellant storage and the flights that fill it before an HLS departure.
RENDERING
Artist’s concept — not a photograph of flown hardware.
